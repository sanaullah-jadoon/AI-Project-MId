About

BFS — level by level. Finds fewest hops, ignores weights.

DFS — deep first. Fast but non-optimal.

UCS — lowest cost first. Always optimal.

A* — cost + heuristic. Near-optimal, fewest nodes.

Hill Climbing — greedy local. Gets stuck at local max (failure case demonstrated).

Minimax — attacker vs defender. Full tree.

Alpha-Beta — Minimax + pruning. Same result, fewer nodes.
